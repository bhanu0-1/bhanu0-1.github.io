Most recently I handled escalated cases, fraud detection and identity-theft reporting on the NatWest Group account at Teleperformance UK, working intake, chat and email queues inside a highly regulated, FCA-compliant environment.

That case-investigation instinct — look for the pattern, flag the anomaly, explain it clearly — is what carried into my analytics work. Outside client work, I build end-to-end analytics projects — from raw Kaggle data to a finished Power BI dashboard — to keep my SQL, Python and DAX sharp.
